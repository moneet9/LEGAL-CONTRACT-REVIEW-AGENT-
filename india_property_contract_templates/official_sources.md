# Official sources

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| NGDRS (Govt. of India, DoLR) state-wise deed templates, e.g. Punjab Sale Deed | https://ngdrs.gov.in/NGDRS_Website/templates/punjab/Sale_Deed_template.pdf |
| Delhi Revenue Dept. model deeds: Sale Deed, Agreement to Sell, Lease Deed, Exchange Deed, Will, GPA, Gift Deed | https://dmeast.delhi.gov.in/model-deed-page/ |
| Puducherry "Registration - Format for various Deeds" | https://www.py.gov.in/registration-format-various-deeds-0 |
| Model Tenancy Act 2021 (MoHUA) - applies where a State/UT adopts it | https://mohua.gov.in |
| Central Acts (text) | https://www.indiacode.nic.in : Transfer of Property Act 1882, Registration Act 1908, Indian Stamp Act 1899, Indian Succession Act 1925, Powers of Attorney Act 1882, Indian Contract Act 1872 |
| State stamp duty, registration, e-stamp | Your State's Registration & Stamps / IGR portal (e.g. Maharashtra IGR, Karnataka Kaveri, Tamil Nadu TNREGINET, Delhi DoRIS) and SHCIL e-stamping |
| Case law behind the "GPA sale" red flag | Suraj Lamp & Industries v. State of Haryana (Supreme Court, 2011) |

Which template follows which source: see `sources` inside each `templates/*.json`.
Only the Punjab NGDRS sale-deed format was read in full while building this pack; the Delhi model
deeds were listed but their contents could not be opened, so verify against them directly.
