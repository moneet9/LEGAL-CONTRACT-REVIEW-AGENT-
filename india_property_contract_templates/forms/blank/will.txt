LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT

I, [TESTATOR NAME], S/o / D/o / W/o [NAME], aged about [AGE] years, [RELIGION], resident of [ADDRESS], PAN [PAN],
being of sound and disposing mind and in good health, and making this Will of my own free will without any coercion, undue influence or pressure,
do hereby revoke all my previous wills and codicils and declare this to be my last Will.

1. EXECUTOR: I appoint [EXECUTOR NAME], residing at [ADDRESS], as the executor of this Will.
2. DEBTS AND EXPENSES: My debts, funeral and testamentary expenses shall be paid out of my estate.
3. SPECIFIC BEQUESTS: I bequeath the following properties absolutely:
   (a) the property described in Schedule A ([DESCRIPTION]) to [BENEFICIARY 1], [RELATIONSHIP];
   (b) the property described in Schedule B ([DESCRIPTION]) to [BENEFICIARY 2], [RELATIONSHIP];
   (c) my bank accounts, shares and jewellery, being [DETAILS], to [BENEFICIARY 3].
4. ALTERNATE BENEFICIARY: If any beneficiary predeceases me, his/her share shall pass to [ALTERNATE BENEFICIARY].
5. GUARDIAN: If any beneficiary is a minor, [GUARDIAN NAME] shall act as guardian of his/her property until majority.
6. RESIDUARY CLAUSE: The rest and remainder of my property of every kind shall go to [RESIDUARY BENEFICIARY].
7. This Will takes effect after my death.

SCHEDULE A / B: [PROPERTY DESCRIPTIONS WITH SURVEY NO., ADDRESS, AREA, BOUNDARIES]

IN WITNESS WHEREOF I have signed this Will at [PLACE] on this [DAY] day of [MONTH] [YEAR].
TESTATOR: [SIGNATURE / THUMB IMPRESSION]
